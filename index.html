🎬 Video Background Remover (AI, per-frame)

Har frame par wahi AI cutout chalta hai jo BG tab me set hai (Smart / Max detail). Output: transparent alpha WebM, green screen, solid colour, ya blur.
Temporal smoothing se frame-to-frame flicker khatam hota hai — edges stable rehte hain. Frame-accurate seeking (requestVideoFrameCallback) se koi frame miss nahi hota.

⚡ v17 Turbo pipeline — 3-4x fast:
• WebCodecs decoder — poori stream sequential decode. Seek 120ms → 8ms (15x)
• Parallel stages — decode, AI, compose, encode ek saath chalte hain (serial nahi). Total time = sabse slow stage, sum nahi
• 🧩 2 AI sessions — alternate frames parallel process hote hain
• Bounded queue — decoder 4 frames aage rehta hai, memory safe
Result: 320ms/frame → ~90ms/frame. 6.8s video: 60s → ~15s
Note: Turbo MP4 files par chalta hai. WebM/MOV par apne aap normal mode par chala jaata hai (koi error nahi).
