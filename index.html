AI Watermark / Text Remover (batch inpainting)

🔤 Multi-pass OCR: image ke 6 variants par OCR chalta hai — original, CLAHE contrast-boost, inverted, 2x upscale+sharpen, adaptive binarize, inverted-binarize. Faint, low-contrast, chhota, light-on-dark — har tarah ka text pakadta hai. Results IoU se dedupe hote hain.
🔁 Iterative removal: text hatane ke baad dobara scan hota hai — jo residue bacha wo agle round me hatta hai (2-3 rounds).
🤚 Skin-aware: pehle skin par ka poora box reject ho jaata tha (isliye haath ke paas ka text bach jaata tha). Ab skin par bhi text hataate hain — sirf uske stroke pixels. Face features alag se protect hain.
Stroke-level mask: poora rectangle nahi mitaya jaata — box ke andar sirf text ke stroke pixels nikaale jaate hain (dual-Otsu se). Letters ke beech ka asli photo bilkul safe rehta hai.
🔒 Pixel-exact guarantee: output original ki byte-for-byte copy se banta hai aur sirf mask > 0 wale pixels replace hote hain. Baaki har pixel bilkul original. Log me exact % dikhta hai ki kitne pixel badle.
🛡 Face & skin protect upar se aur, aur 👁 Preview se pehle dekh lo.
